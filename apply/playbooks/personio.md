# Personio

Read `_common.md` first. This ATS never auto-submits: fill everything, then stop `READY`.

- Hosted on `*.jobs.personio.de` or `*.jobs.personio.com`. The form sits below the job text; click "Apply now" if
  present. Dismiss the cookie banner first.
- Standard fields: first and last name, email, phone, location, start date, salary expectation, documents (CV, cover
  letter, other), optional message. Salary and start date come from `answers.json`.
- Cover letter or message box: follow the cover-letter rule in `_common.md`.
- Tick the privacy and consent checkbox at the bottom. Upload with `browser_file_upload`; verify filenames.
- The form language may be German. Translate each question, match it to `answers.json`, and continue.
