# SmartRecruiters

Read `_common.md` first. This ATS never auto-submits: fill everything, then stop `READY`.

- Posting pages (`jobs.smartrecruiters.com/...`) have an "I'm interested" or "Apply" button leading to the form.
  Dismiss the cookie banner first.
- Upload the resume first: fields prefill. Re-check every prefilled value against `answers.json`.
- Multi-step form: contact information, experience, education, questions, review. Include all employments and
  educations, most recent first.
- Country, phone and location fields are autocompletes: type, wait ~800ms, read the options, click the exact one.
- Cover letter or message box: follow the cover-letter rule in `_common.md`.
- Tick the consent boxes on the last page. Verify filenames before the review step.
